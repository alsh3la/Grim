Four tools. One clean interface.

Everything Grim does is built around one idea: getting to your stuff should take zero effort.

File Explorer

Browse local storage with a clean, fast file tree. Move, rename, search, and preview — no clutter, no lag.

Google Drive Login

Sign in with your Google account to access Drive files directly. Secure OAuth — Grim never sees your password.

Music Player

Scan your folders and play local audio with a built-in player. Background playback, playlists, and no ads.

Backup

Schedule automatic backups of selected folders to Drive or local storage. Restore in a tap whenever you need to.
