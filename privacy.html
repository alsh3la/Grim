Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Grim ("Grim", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Grim application and website (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Google account data. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and a Google account identifier.
  • Google Drive access. If you grant Drive permissions, we access only the files and folders you explicitly authorize through Google's OAuth consent screen.
  • Support communications. Any information you include when you contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data. App version, device type, operating system, and general feature usage, used to diagnose crashes and improve the Service.
  • Local file metadata. File names, sizes, and folder structure are read on your device to power the File Explorer and Music features. This metadata is processed locally and is not uploaded unless you enable backup.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to your Google Drive files.
  • To operate core features: file browsing, music playback, and backup.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate service-related notices.
  • To understand aggregate usage trends so we can improve Grim.

3. Google User Data

Grim's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or ad personalization.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google Drive content, except where you explicitly request support and grant permission, or where required by law.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these cases:

  • Service providers. Trusted vendors who host infrastructure or provide crash reporting, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements. Where required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data in transit is protected using TLS. Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure storage. Backups are encrypted at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. You may disconnect Google Drive access at any time from within the app or from your Google Account permissions page. When you delete your Grim account, we delete associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and portability. Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction. Update inaccurate account information at any time.
  • Deletion. Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Revoke access. Remove Grim's access to your Google account via Google Account permissions.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@grim.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Grim is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email privacy@grim.app.
